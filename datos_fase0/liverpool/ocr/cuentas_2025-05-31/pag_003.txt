Review of the business /
During the reporting period the Club were successful in winning The Premier League and clinching the Club’s 20%
English league title and by virtue qualified for the Champions League competition for Season 2025/26. The Club
were knocked out in the 4" round of The FA Cup, EFL Cup runners-up, and exited the UEFA Champions League in
the Round of 16 during Season 2024/25.
‘Profit and Loss Account
Turnover for the year ended 31 May 2025 was £702.8 million (2024: £613.8 million).
Media revenue for the year ended 31 May 2025 was £263.7 million (2024: £203.7 million). The increase mainly
related to the Club’s participation in the Champions League competition in Season 2024/25 compared to the Club’s
participation in the Europa League competition in Season 2023/24. This was in addition to increased Premier
. League Media revenue as a result of stronger on pitch performance. ,
Match day revenue for the year ended 31 May 2025 was £115.6 million (2024: £101.7 million). The increase in
matchday revenue was as a result of the Anfield Road stand being operational throughout the season and a higher
number of competitive games being played in Season 2024/25.
Commercial revenue for the year ended 31 May 2025 was £323.5 million (2024: £308.4 million), with strong ,
growth in sponsorship and retail revenue during this period.
