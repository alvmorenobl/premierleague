At the year end the Club held a £350.0 million revolving credit facility, of which £69.0 million had been drawn
down. On 24 September 2024, the Group refinanced its credit facility which was due to mature in July 2025. This
provided £350.0 million of facilities for a term of five years and is available for general corporate purposes
including working capital and letters of credit. The Group’s forecasts and projections show that the Group should be
able to operate within the level of the credit facility and meet its obligations as they fall due in the foreseeable
future.

The £217.9 million (2024: £198.7 million) due to group undertaking at 31 May 2025 represented an intercompany
creditor with FSG Football Group, LLC. This intercompany loan has been provided to support broader commercial
and football strategic growth projects.
